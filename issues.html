The Issues

Who Decides?

PresidentCongress
Commander in Chief2Declares war1
Negotiates treatiesSenate approves them (2/3 vote)2
Directs the militaryFunds the military

The War Powers Resolution (1973) gives the President 60 days of combat before Congress must approve.3 Presidents of both parties have worked around it.

The Iran War (2026)

  • Feb 28: U.S. and Israeli strikes on Iran begin.4
  • May 1: The 60-day deadline passes. No vote.5
  • June 23: Senate votes 50–48 to pull U.S. forces out.6
  • August: A peace agreement collapses.4
  • Sept 24: Another Senate resolution fails, 49–50.7

The Problems

No vote

Congress never authorized the war.5

High cost

$38B in military costs and about $2,000 per household.4, 8

Public left out

No vote means no real public debate.

Our Solutions

  1. Oversight: Congress votes before war, and old authorizations expire. Congress repealed two Iraq authorizations in 2025, so it can be done.17
  2. Diplomacy first: negotiate before using force.
  3. Transparency: publish where we're fighting and what it costs.
