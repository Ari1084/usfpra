Organizations we plan to approach

Organizations We Plan to Approach

A student group grows faster with experienced allies. These are the organizations we plan to reach out to. None of them has agreed to partner with us yet.

Student network

John Quincy Adams Society

A nonpartisan student network focused on foreign policy restraint. Its chapters host speakers and debates but stay out of activism and elections, so we would work with them on education events.

Lobbying & training

Friends Committee on National Legislation

A nonpartisan Quaker lobby in Washington that trains everyday constituents to lobby Congress. Its training would help prepare our members for lobby day.

Think tank

Quincy Institute for Responsible Statecraft

A think tank focused on restraint that supports strengthening Congress's war powers. Its research could support our talking points.

Research

Defense Priorities

A research organization focused on a restrained U.S. foreign policy and a strong, secure defense.

War powers coalition

Groups From the Left and the Right

We plan to approach groups across the political spectrum that work on war powers and government accountability, so our coalition is clearly nonpartisan: Win Without War, Common Defense, Concerned Veterans for America, the Project On Government Oversight, and Protect Democracy.
