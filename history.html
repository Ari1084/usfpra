Lessons From History

Best vs. Worst Decisions

As ranked by historians of U.S. foreign policy.14

Best

Marshall Plan · United Nations · NATO · Cuban Missile Crisis diplomacy

Worst

Iraq invasion (#1) · Vietnam combat troops (#2) · Gulf of Tonkin Resolution

The best decisions used diplomacy. The worst were wars.

Vietnam

  • Started with a rushed 1964 vote based on bad information27
  • 58,220 Americans killed10
  • $686 billion in 2008 dollars11
  • The government misled the public (Pentagon Papers)12
  • Goal not reached: Saigon fell in 197513

Iraq

  • Sold on weapons of mass destruction that didn't exist15
  • 4,488 U.S. troops killed16
  • $2.2 trillion, 44 times the original estimate16
  • Its war authorization lasted 20+ years17
