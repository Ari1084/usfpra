Where we plan to be this year, and why

Event Calendar

Every event is built around one question: should Congress vote before America goes to war? All events are nonpartisan and open to students of any political view.

Colleges We Plan to Reach

We are starting with campuses in the Washington, D.C. area, close to Congress.

George Mason University Veterans Day & Selective Service events

Nearly 14% of its 40,000+ students are current or former service members or military dependents, so questions about war hit close to home.29 Active student political clubs make it a good fit for nonpartisan forums.

George Washington University Lobby day

Located in D.C., minutes from Congress, and home to a John Quincy Adams Society chapter recognized in 2023.30

University of Maryland, College Park Debate night

A large public campus with active party clubs, a strong match for our debate night.

American University & Georgetown University Teach-ins

Both have large international affairs programs full of students who already follow foreign policy. (We have not confirmed whether either has a restraint-focused chapter.)

Johns Hopkins, William & Mary & Gettysburg College Partner chapters

All three have had John Quincy Adams Society chapters we could work with.30 Gettysburg also holds an annual student policy debate between campus political groups, which we can use as a model for our own debate night.31

Community colleges: NOVA, Prince George's & Montgomery College Selective Service events

These schools serve many students ages 18 to 25, the exact group affected by automatic Selective Service registration.
