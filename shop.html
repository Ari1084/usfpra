Meet Our Mascot

Our mascot is an owl holding a gavel. The owl stands for wisdom and careful thinking before acting. The gavel stands for Congress and the rule of law, since the Constitution gives Congress the power to declare war.

We used navy, gold, and cream on purpose. Red and blue together can look like one political party, and USFPRA is nonpartisan.

Name: we're still deciding. Our current pick is highlighted.

Where Your Purchase Goes

Merch sales would fund our campus outreach and advocacy: booths, flyers, events, social media, and trips to meet with members of Congress. See the full breakdown on our Get Involved page.

Merch also helps spread the message. Every sticker on a laptop or shirt on campus is a conversation starter about who decides when America goes to war.

This is a class project. The products above are planned designs, and nothing is actually sold through this website.
