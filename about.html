About Us

Our Mission

"USFPRA promotes thoughtful limits, diplomacy, and public accountability in U.S. foreign policy."

What Kind of Group Are We?

Public-Interest

War affects every American, so better decisions help everyone, not just our members.

Ideological

We share one belief: restraint. Use force carefully, and respect the Constitution's checks. We're nonpartisan.

Foreign Policy

Our whole focus is how the U.S. deals with the world: war, diplomacy, trade, and security.

What We Believe

  • Diplomacy and trade first.
  • Stay out of conflicts that don't threaten the U.S.
  • Force only when necessary, like defending the country or our allies.
  • Congress votes first. It hasn't declared war since 1942.21
