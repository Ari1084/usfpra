Who We Are

USFPRA is a public-interest, ideological, and foreign policy interest group. We push for a more restrained U.S. foreign policy: one where Congress and the public have a real say before the country goes to war, and where diplomacy comes first.

Our mission: USFPRA promotes thoughtful limits, diplomacy, and public accountability in U.S. foreign policy.

What We Stand For

Trade & Economy

Strong trade relationships keep Americans employed and goods affordable. Wars disrupt trade and raise prices at home, like the spike in gas prices since the Iran conflict began.

Diplomacy & Oversight

The Constitution gives Congress the power to declare war. We want Congress to use it, and we want negotiations and alliances tried before force.

Security & Peace

We are not against defending the country. Military action should stay on the table for real threats to national security, decided openly and with a clear goal.

We've Been Here Before

Historians ranked the 2003 invasion of Iraq and the 1965 decision to send combat troops to Vietnam as the two worst foreign policy decisions in U.S. history.14 Both wars started with rushed decisions, cost far more than promised, and did not reach their goals.

The same survey ranked the Marshall Plan, the creation of the United Nations, and NATO among the best. Those were built on diplomacy and economic partnership.

Lessons From History

College Students Have the Most at Stake

Starting in December 2026, men ages 18 to 25 will be automatically registered for the Selective Service.20 The debt from today's wars will be paid by our generation. We think young people deserve a voice in these decisions.
