The lore

Who is Tung Tung Tung Sahur?

A wide-eyed wooden figure with a baseball bat who became one of the biggest memes of 2025. Here's where he came from.

01

The sound is real

In Indonesia during Ramadan, neighbors wake each other before dawn for sahur, the meal eaten before a day of fasting. Groups walk the streets knocking on drums and wooden kentongan. Tung, tung, tung.

02

The internet made him a log

In early 2025, AI-generated videos on TikTok turned that knock into a character: a log with huge eyes, a bat, and a voice chanting “tung” over and over before yelling “sahur.”

03

He joined a universe

Tung became a star of the “Italian brainrot” wave of absurd AI characters with chantable names. Fans remix him in edits, games, and fan art.
